## The project

[MOSAICO]({{ '/projects/mosaico/' | relative_url }}) is a toolkit and corpus
for analyzing the writing of heritage speakers of Spanish: people who grew up
with Spanish at home in an English-dominant society. It describes how these
writers combine their languages instead of counting departures from a
monolingual standard as errors. The toolkit is built one part at a time, and
each semester a new cohort of interns takes it a step further.

## Roles

Each cohort brings together three people with different backgrounds who work

## The first semester

The Spring 2027 cohort lays the groundwork for the whole project:

- Assemble a pilot corpus of writing samples contributed by UNR students.
- Build and test Module 1, which tags each word for language and finds the
  points where a writer switches between Spanish and English.
- Document the work so the next cohort can build on it.

## What you gain

- Experience on a research project from data collection to evaluation.
- Practice with language processing and corpus tools on real data.
- Mentoring from faculty in Hispanic and computational linguistics.
- A concrete team project to describe in résumés and graduate school applications.

## Who should apply

UNR undergraduates in Spanish, linguistics, computer science, data science and
related programs, and students in the heritage language program. The roles
above describe the background each position calls for.
