## MOSAICO

<p class="project__status">{{ site.data.mosaico.expansion }}. First intern cohort in Spring 2027.</p>

A modular toolkit and corpus for analyzing the writing of heritage speakers of
Spanish. MOSAICO describes how bilingual writers combine their languages, from
code-switching to contact and regional features, and is built by cohorts of
undergraduate interns.
