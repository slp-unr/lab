The SLP Lab is starting MOSAICO (*Multilingual Output and Sociolinguistic
Analysis in Contact*), a project on the computational analysis of writing by
heritage speakers of Spanish.

MOSAICO describes how bilingual writers combine their languages, from
code-switching to contact-induced structures and regional features, instead of
treating their writing as a set of errors measured against a monolingual
standard. The toolkit is organized in six modules, and the first one tags each
word for language and finds the points where a writer switches between Spanish
and English.

The project will be built by cohorts of paid undergraduate interns. For Spring
2027 we are looking for three UNR undergraduates: a developer, a linguist and a
heritage language specialist. Applications are open through the
[online form]({{ site.data.mosaico.apply_url }}).
