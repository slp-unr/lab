<h3 class="project-name">MOSAICO</h3>
<p class="project-expansion">{{ site.data.mosaico.expansion }}</p>

MOSAICO is a modular toolkit and corpus for analyzing the writing of heritage
speakers of Spanish: people who grew up with Spanish at home in an
English-dominant society. Instead of counting errors against a monolingual
standard, it describes the full repertoire their writing shows, from
code-switching to regional and contact features.
